About Us

POINT Coffee Space is a loft-style specialty coffee shop located in Soi Num Uthit, Hat Yai. Decorated with raw concrete walls and black steel structures, it offers a cool and unique atmosphere.

Our baristas are happy to recommend menus tailored to your taste. Whether you prefer medium or dark roast, hot or iced, we have it all. Enjoy our air-conditioned indoor area or the lush outdoor garden.
